# Delete your Koltz Sports account

This page is for parents, guardians, adult players and coaches who use the **Koltz Sports** app
(developer: [to be added before release]).

## In the app

Open the app → **Profile → Delete account** → enter your password → confirm. You are signed out on all
devices at once.

## Without the app

E-mail [to be added before release] from any address with the subject "Delete my account". Include your
**registered mobile number** and your academy's name. We confirm by contacting that mobile number before
deleting anything, so nobody else can delete your account.

## What is deleted, and when

| Data | What happens |
|---|---|
| Your login: mobile number, username, password, devices, notification settings | Deleted within 30 days |
| Your name and contact details as a parent | Deleted within 30 days, except where your academy still needs them for a child who remains enrolled (the academy will speak to you) |
| Your child's academy record (attendance, batches) | Kept by the academy while the child is enrolled. To erase it, send a privacy request for the child. Once the child leaves, the profile is made anonymous after 12 months |
| Fee bills, payments and receipts | Kept for as long as tax law requires, with as few personal details as possible |
| App access purchases (Google Play order numbers) | Kept for as long as tax law requires |
| Backups | Overwritten within 30 days |

Deleting your account does not cancel or refund app access automatically. See the Refund Policy
(https://recurrrsion-tech.github.io/koltz-site/refunds/).
