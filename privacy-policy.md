# Koltz Sports — Privacy Policy

**Effective:** not yet in effect (draft) · **Version:** 1.0-draft

Koltz Sports ("Koltz", "we") is an app that cricket academies in India use to run admissions,
batches, attendance, fees, appointments and match announcements. Parents and adult players use it to
follow their own or their child's activity at the academy. Koltz is operated by [to be added before release],
[to be added before release].

Most students are children. This policy explains, in plain words, what we collect, why, who sees it, and
what you can ask us to do. It is written to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023
and the Rules under it.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

- **Your academy** decides what records it keeps about its students, parents and coaches: admissions, batches, attendance, fee bills and receipts, appointments and matches. For these records the academy is the **Data Fiduciary**, and Koltz processes them on the academy's behalf and on its instructions.
- **Koltz** is responsible for your **login account**, the **₹499 app access** you may buy from us, and any support request you send us. For these, Koltz is the Data Fiduciary.
- If you are not sure whom to ask, ask us. We will pass your request to your academy when it is theirs to decide.

## 2. What we collect

| About | What | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| Parent or guardian | Name, mobile number, relationship to the child, e-mail (optional), password (stored only as a secure hash) | Name and mobile required |
| Student | Name, date of birth, gender (optional), batch and age group, admission number, photograph (optional), emergency contact (optional), playing role and batting/bowling hand (optional), medical or injury notes (optional, see §4) | Name and date of birth required |
| Adult player (18+) | The same as a student, plus their own mobile number and password | |
| Coach or staff | Name, mobile number, photograph (optional), batches they coach | |
| Activity | Attendance and check-in times (from the student's QR ID card), absence reasons, fee bills, payments and receipts, UPI reference numbers you report for coaching fees, appointments, match availability replies, notifications sent | |
| Consent | That a guardian consented for a child, when, which version of this policy, and how the academy verified the guardian | |
| App access purchase | From Google Play: order number, purchase token, product, date, price, refund status. **We never receive your card, bank or UPI details for this purchase** | |
| Device | Push notification token, app version, device model and Android version, and error reports that contain no personal details | |

We do **not** collect Aadhaar or other ID numbers, location, contacts, or anything from your phone
that the app does not need.

## 3. Why we use it

- To run the academy's work: admit students, place them in batches, mark attendance, bill fees and issue receipts, book coach appointments, announce matches.
- To let you log in, keep your account secure, and show you only your own or your child's records.
- To send notifications you need: check-in alerts, absences, bills, receipts, appointments, matches, closures.
- To sell, verify and manage app access bought through Google Play.
- To answer your requests and complaints, keep audit logs of sensitive actions, and meet legal duties (for example, tax records).

We use data **only** for these purposes. We do not sell it, rent it, or use it for advertising.

## 4. Children

- A child's record is created only after a parent or guardian gives **consent**, which the academy verifies. We record that consent.
- Children under 18 do not get their own login. The parent's login covers them.
- **No advertising, no tracking, no profiling.** We do not track children's behaviour, monitor them for advertising, or show them ads. There are no advertising or analytics companies in the app.
- Medical or injury notes are optional. Only the academy admin, the child's own coaches and the parent can see them.
- The student's ID card shows the academy's details and the student's photo, name, admission number, batch and a QR code. The QR holds a random code, not personal details. The card shows no date of birth, mobile number or address.

## 5. Who can see what

- **Academy admin:** everything in their own academy.
- **Coach:** only the students in the batches they coach, without fee details.
- **Parent:** only their own children. **Adult player:** only their own record.
- **Other academies** cannot see your data.
- **Koltz staff:** only to fix a problem the academy or you reported, and every such access is logged.

We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

## 7. Where and how it is kept safe

Data is stored in India (AWS Mumbai), encrypted in storage and in transit, with nightly encrypted
backups. Access is limited by role and logged. Passwords are hashed (argon2id).

## 8. How long we keep it

- While the student is enrolled and the academy uses Koltz.
- When a student leaves: their profile is kept for 12 months and then made anonymous.
- Fee, payment and receipt records: for as long as tax law requires, with as few personal details as possible.
- App access purchase records: for as long as tax law requires.
- Audit logs: at least one year.
- If an academy stops using Koltz, it gets its data back and we delete our copies (including backups) within 30 days.

## 9. Your rights

You can ask to:

- **see** the data held about you or your child, and with whom it was shared;
- **correct or complete** it;
- **erase** it (we may keep what the law requires, such as financial records);
- **withdraw consent** (the academy will then speak to you about what happens to your child's enrolment);
- **nominate** someone to act for you if you die or cannot act;
- **complain** (§12).

Ask in the app (**Profile → Privacy requests**), or write to us (§12). These requests are free and are
**never** blocked because app access has not been bought. We reply within 30 days.

## 10. Deleting your account

In the app: **Profile → Delete account**. Without the app: see https://recurrrsion-tech.github.io/koltz-site/account-deletion/.

## 11. If something goes wrong

If personal data is lost or seen by someone who should not have seen it, we tell the academy and the
people affected, and report to the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.
