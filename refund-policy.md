# Koltz Sports — Refund and Cancellation Policy

**Effective:** not yet in effect (draft) · **Version:** 1.0-draft

This policy covers **app access** (₹499 per student per year), bought in the Koltz Sports app through
Google Play. **Coaching fees** are paid to your academy, not to Koltz. For refunds of coaching fees,
ask your academy.

## R1. Free period

Your academy may have a free period. Nothing is charged during it.

## R2. Cancelling

App access is a **one-time purchase for one year**. It does not renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel. When the year ends, access stops unless you buy again.

## R3. When we refund

| Situation | Refund |
|---|---|
| You ask within **7 days** of buying | Full refund |
| You bought access twice for the same child by mistake | Full refund of the extra purchase |
| Access did not work because of a fault on our side and we could not fix it within 7 days of your report | Full refund |
| Your academy stops using Koltz, or Koltz closes, during your access year | Refund for the **unused full months** |
| Your child leaves the academy, or you stop using the app, more than 7 days after buying | No refund. Access continues until the end of the year |

## R4. How to ask

- **Within 48 hours of buying:** you can also ask Google directly. Open Google Play → Profile → Payments and subscriptions → Budget and history, choose the purchase, and request a refund. Google decides these requests.
- **Any time:** write to [to be added before release] with your registered mobile number, the child's name and the Google Play order number (it starts with "GPA." and is in Google's e-mail receipt).

We reply within 2 working days and decide within 7 working days.

## R5. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the payment method you used in Google Play. Google processes them, usually in a few
working days, depending on your bank. When a purchase is refunded, that year of access ends.
